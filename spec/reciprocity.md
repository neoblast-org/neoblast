# Storage reciprocity (draft, Phase 3)

See decision 0005. Not implemented yet.

- **Swap:** when B stores shard *s* for A, A stores an equal-sized claim for B.
  `claim(B, j) = BLAKE3.keyed(K_claim_B).xof(seek = j · chunk)` — incompressible, regenerable by B only.
- **Challenge:** "send chunk *j* of what you hold for me". A shard chunk is checked
  against its BLAKE3 chunk proof; a claim chunk is regenerated by its owner.
- **Punishment:** after the *i*-th consecutive failed challenge, drop the partner's
  data with probability rising with *i* (Samsara, SOSP 2003). Target: an honest node
  offline 8 h/night loses < 1% of swaps a month; a cheater loses all within 7 days.
- **Lease:** 30 days (ROADMAP open item 5), renewed by owner, pinner or donor.
- **Allowance:** about 1 GB free per new node; beyond that, upload ≤ proven storage.
