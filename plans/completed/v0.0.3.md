## Success criteria

1. **Version 0.0.3.** All four crates are at 0.0.3 (`RELEASING.md` step 1),
   and the three lock files change only their `neoblast-*` versions.
2. **Reviewed before the campaign.** Code and security reviews cover
   `805a5ed` to the bump on `main`, with no open blocker, and every fix they
   call for in `code_id` is merged before the campaign starts.
3. **Fuzzed on the final code.** `fuzz/campaign.sh summary` shows `met` ×5
   on the `code_id` of the tagged commit, with the compiler and `cargo-fuzz`
   that CI pins (`nightly-2026-10-14`, 0.13.2), or CI re-pinned to the
   campaign's. Nothing in `code_id` changes once the campaign starts.
4. **The changelog says what shipped.** `CHANGELOG.md` dates 0.0.3 on the UTC
   day of the tag, lists every item above that a user of the crates or the
   CLI can see (all but W-046, a test), and its fuzzing sentence matches the
   records. Its footer gains a `[0.0.3]` link, and `[Unreleased]` compares
   from `v0.0.3`.
5. **Released like v0.0.2.**
   - **Gate:** the release commit logs the last reviews and changes only
     `docs/logs/`, `plans/` and `CHANGELOG.md`; its parent is the newest Tip
     in both logs and both baselines; it has one parent; and the commands in
     `RELEASING.md` step 2 print what they must. Its own changes are reviewed
     by a second agent before it merges.
   - **Findings:** no open finding of medium severity or higher is shipped.
     S-027 is not in the released code, so it stays open and private
     (`SECURITY.md`); step 2's security review confirms that this still holds.
   - **Merges:** every merge is a fast-forward of signed project commits that
     GitHub shows as verified; PRs are opened and merged only as
     `neoblast-dev`.
   - **Publish:** the signed tag passes the hygiene job and the Release
     workflow's checks; the crates go out through trusted publishing.
   - **Release:** the signed tag and the GitHub release are by `neoblast-dev`,
     and the release is published only after the `publish` job succeeds and
     crates.io lists all four crates at 0.0.3.
